\chapter{Introduction}
\label{cha:introduction}

Plan for the intro:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Start general: condensed matter, solid state physics, materials and properties
    \item Electronic and Optical properties interplay in materials
    \item Exotic physical phenomena, interactions: Bose-Einstein Condensate, Super conductivity, mott insulators, strange metals, topological phases of matter, etc.
    \item Special case for \bd~materials, exotic phenomena, interplay between electronic, magnetic, optical, thermal, mechanical (super lubridicity?)
    \item Particular emphasis on electronic and optical one; light--matter interactions, imprint of many-body physics in experimental observations, measuring absorption spectra, photoluminescense, excitons;
    \item Strong-coupling light--matter, exciton plus light forms exciton--polariton
    \item Polaritonics and field of photonics, general remarks
    \item hBN as exotic material in its bulk form (anisotropy, hyperbolicity), phonon excitations in the mid-IR, can be tailored for UV applications as single-layer, UV spectra of materials not very well understood or established in the literature;
    \item material response differs from frequency range to frequency range, above bandgap single-particle, subgap reveals many-body effects;
    \item many-body problem is hard, approximations to get results and infer conclusions easily, also allows understanding what happens;
    \item Outline of the thesis;
    \item Vocabulary: prospects
\end{itemize}
