\chapter{Outlook and Conclusions}
\label{ch:Outlook}

As a final say in this dissertation, after all the concepts from the literature reviewed throughout the thesis alongside their readaptation for studying light--matter interactions in insulating \bd~materials, there is pertinence in providing a summarized overview of what was discussed in each chapter.
Throughout the course of the PhD studies, and mainly from the research performed, novel ideas naturally arised for which there was not enough time to develop.
After the overview of all the chapters one by one, we will have the opportunity to briefly discuss those ideas.

\noindent
\textbf{\hyperref[ch:classical_electrodynamics]{Chapter 2\,} \textcolor{blue}{|} Classical Electrodynamics of Continuous Media}

This chapter had the purpose of providing a simple, but complete, technical introduction for the reader, within the classical framework for electrodynamics in solids and continuous media.
Several key concepts in this work were introduced, specifically those that can quantify light--matter interactions through material response functions.
More specifically, we highlighted the concepts of dielectric function and conductivity in optically responsive systems, together with their equivalence relations.
The physics related to electromagnetic phenomena at planar interfaces between different media was also discussed, with the intent of contextualizing \bd~materials, the main topic of this thesis.
As these materials present many phenomena of interest at nanometric scales that comes from quantum effects, the tools herein introduced are deemed insufficient.
The necessary tools are provided in the two chapters that follow.
